# Code of Conduct

VYRA is a research project. We want people with any level of experience in
cryptography or Rust to be able to ask questions, report problems and send
changes without being made to feel unwelcome.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, results and code, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and give technical criticism with reasons and evidence.
- Accept correction. In cryptography, being shown wrong is how the work
  improves; nobody here is expected to be right the first time.
- Be patient with newcomers. A question that looks basic is still welcome.
- Keep discussion on the project: its code, its claims and its evidence.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or
  imagery of any kind.
- Sexual language or advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and
anywhere else a person is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers, [@Encryptic1](https://github.com/Encryptic1)
and [@treyhuffine](https://github.com/treyhuffine), by contacting either of them
directly through GitHub. If the report concerns one maintainer, contact the
other. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that
break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for
behaviour they judge harmful.
